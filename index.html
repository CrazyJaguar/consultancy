FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Budget varies by country. Germany has tuition-free public universities (only living costs ~€800/month). Australia ranges from AUD 20,000–45,000/year. Canada costs CAD 20,000–40,000/year. We'll find the best fit for your budget.
Most English-speaking countries (Australia, Canada, UK) require IELTS/PTE. Germany accepts TOEFL or IELTS. Some programs accept MOI (Medium of Instruction) letters instead. We assess your specific university requirements during counselling.
Australia student visa typically takes 4–6 weeks. Canada takes 8–12 weeks. Germany takes 6–8 weeks. We recommend starting the process at least 4 months before your intended intake date.
Yes! Australia allows 48 hours/fortnight. Canada allows 20 hours/week. Germany allows 120 full days/year. UK allows 20 hours/week during term. Work rights are factored into our financial planning for you.
Many excellent options: Australia Awards, Destination Australia, Chevening (UK), DAAD (Germany), Erasmus+, and university-specific merit scholarships. We have successfully secured scholarships ranging from 20% to full tuition waivers for our students.
